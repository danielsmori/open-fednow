# Code of Conduct

OpenFedNow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep discussion on the technical subject, and do not harass or demean anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@danielsmori](https://github.com/danielsmori), privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer will review each report and may warn, restrict or remove a participant, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
